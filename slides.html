What is this
TVHL in one line

TVHL is the operating system for a loan officer's book of business — it turns a rate inquiry into live, investor-accurate pricing, sends a branded quote and proposal in one click, and keeps every borrower conversation, scenario and quote in one consolidated thread.

Why it's needed
Two gaps a spreadsheet and inbox can't close

The pricing gap

Pulling accurate, investor-specific pricing across every rate and lock period is slow and error-prone — LOs re-run searches by hand, and a stale number costs the deal or the margin.

The continuity gap

Borrower conversations scatter across email, phone and text. Scenarios get lost, follow-up is slow, and neither the LO nor the borrower has a single trustworthy view of where things stand.

Today this runs on Optimal Blue tabs, spreadsheets and email — it's slow, unauditable, and leaks borrowers between the inquiry and the lock. TVHL closes both gaps in one place.
